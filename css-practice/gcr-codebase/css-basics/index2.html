About Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital city of Rajasthan and is popularly known as the Pink City. It is famous for its beautiful palaces, forts, markets and rich history.

Culture and Heritage

Jaipur has a colorful culture with traditional music, dance, art, food and festivals. The city is well known for its historical architecture and handicrafts.

Popular Places

Some famous attractions include Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace and Jantar Mantar.

Travel Tips

The best way to enjoy Jaipur is to explore its historical places, local markets and traditional food.
